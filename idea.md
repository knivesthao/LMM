Problem: Laos has a serious lack of reading materials for language learners (English and Lao); books are difficult to acquire especially for 

Let's make an app that generates comics, interactive children books, that will be directly publishable to a Media Library accessible to Lao citizens (through a Lao phone number). This will have two parts.

There will be three audiences:

Admin (this group manages the apps)

Users (this group is the target customer)

Publishers/Creators (this group makes the media content)

Our app aims to solve many things: 
-access to educational materials
-lack of reading books
-lack of digital media library
-create new jobs for content creators



1) a web app that creates the media
-uses unreal engine (we need this for the grant)
-users can upload an initial image that will be the base of the media
-slowly build out the media (let's start with three)
--comics: users will be able to narrate the story strip by strip. AI will read through the contents of each strip and generate images
--interactive books (books with short animation clips): users will be able to narrate scene by scene, AI will generate the scenes
-media needs to be light as they will be downloaded, some users will be in remote areas where internet is limited. media should be designed that it can be downloaded in small parts at a time due to the limitations in internet access

2) a digital media library that users can use to download the media
-Laos doesn't support online payments, we will have to use QR payments. (They scan and pay a QR code, send it to a whatsapp number from their number), we credit their number (this should be tied in with their login account), they can purchase books
-users have their own list of media they have purchased
-users need to be able access this even as they are offline
-media needs to be downloadable, as some places have bad internet

*we'll build out a web prototype first, but ultimately this will be a mobile app in Laos, we need support for iOS, android, Chinese app stores, and whatever app stores are popular in Laos

*at first we want to hire people to create digital media for us, eventually they will subscribe to our software as publishers and create content they can sell. subscription has tiers and limits their ability to create content (control AI costs), we will always take a part of the sales. content creators will control the prices

*My partner has a digital media company in Laos, I will have a digital media company in the US. We want to open ourselves to as many grants as possible.
