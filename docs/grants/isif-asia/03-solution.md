# 3. Solution & Hypothesis

## Hypothesis

> *"An AI-powered content creation platform + offline-capable digital library can dramatically increase access to educational reading materials in Laos at lower cost than traditional publishing."*

## Prototype Phase (3 months)

- Build web-based comic creator with basic AI image generation
- Deploy to 50 content creators in Vientiane
- Test: Can creators generate 1 comic strip per day with AI assistance?
- **Gender-balanced testing:** Ensure at least 50% of test creators are women

## Testing Phase (6 months)

- Deploy digital library with QR payment
- 500+ users test offline download functionality
- Measure: reading engagement, content consumption, satisfaction
- **Gender-disaggregated data:** Track usage patterns and outcomes by gender

## Gender Equity

- Content created in both Lao and ethnic minority languages
- Stories feature girls and women as protagonists in non-stereotypical roles
- Female creators recruited and trained as content publishers
- Pricing kept accessible ($0.50-$1.00) — affordable for families regardless of income
