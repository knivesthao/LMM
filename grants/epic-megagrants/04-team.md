# 4. Team

LMM is built by **ADMAIS** — operating as two coordinated companies spanning both sides of this project: one registered in the United States and one registered in Laos. This dual-country structure is not an accident. It is the single most important asset we bring to this project, and the reason we can actually deliver what we are proposing.

## The Structure

| Entity | Location | Role |
|--------|----------|------|
| **ADMAIS (US)** | United States | Technical development, AI pipeline, UE engineering, grant management |
| **ADMAIS (Laos)** | Vientiane, Laos | Creator recruitment, content operations, school partnerships, user testing, payments integration |

Most international development technology projects fail because the team building the technology has never spent a week in the community they are building for. They ship a product that assumes always-on WiFi, credit card payments, and English fluency — and then wonder why it doesn't work in a village in northern Laos.

We do not have this problem. Our Laos office is staffed by Lao nationals who understand the mobile money ecosystem, the telecom infrastructure, the education system, and the cultural context of content consumption. Our US office brings deep technical expertise in Unreal Engine, AI systems, and cloud architecture. When we design the download chunk size for a comic book, we know exactly what a 2G connection feels like in Luang Namtha during rainy season — because our team has tested it there.

## ADMAIS (Laos) — Local Operations

ADMAIS Laos is a digital media and AI services company based in Vientiane, serving small businesses across the country. The team has existing relationships with local telecom providers, familiarity with the Lao QR payment ecosystem (BCEL One, LDB Mobile Banking), and direct experience building digital products for Lao users.

**Key capabilities brought to LMM:**
- On-the-ground user research with Lao students, teachers, and families
- Partnerships with Lao schools and educational institutions for prototype testing
- Integration with Lao mobile payment systems — a domain with zero documentation and tribal knowledge only
- Content creator recruitment and training in Lao language
- Lao-language TTS model fine-tuning and quality assurance
- Navigating the regulatory landscape for digital content distribution in Laos

## ADMAIS (US) — Technical Development

ADMAIS US is a digital media and software company specializing in custom software, AI agent development, and digital growth solutions. The US team brings the Unreal Engine expertise, AI pipeline engineering, and cloud infrastructure capability required to build LMM's core technology.

**Key capabilities brought to LMM:**
- Unreal Engine development — Blueprint scripting, Pixel Streaming deployment, Movie Render Queue pipeline automation
- AI/ML engineering — LLM fine-tuning, Stable Diffusion deployment with IP-Adapter and ControlNet workflows
- Full-stack web development — React, Node.js, REST APIs, PWA architecture
- Cloud infrastructure — AWS GPU orchestration, CDN configuration, cost-optimized auto-scaling
- Grant and project management — experience managing funded technology projects with clear milestones and deliverables

The combination of a US-based technical team and a Laos-based operations team is the precise formula this project requires. We are not parachuting into a developing country with a solution conceived in a Silicon Valley coffee shop.
