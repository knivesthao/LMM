# Grant Proposal Template

Use this template when starting a new grant proposal. Copy the entire `template/` directory, rename it to the grant name, and fill in each section.

## Structure

| # | Section | File | When to Fill |
|---|---------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Problem Statement | `01-problem.md` | Early — the foundation of everything |
| 2 | Solution & Hypothesis | `02-solution.md` | After problem is clear |
| 3 | Innovation Argument | `03-innovation.md` | Mid-stage — differentiator |
| 4 | Team & Human Resources | `04-team.md` | Ongoing — adapt per grant |
| 5 | Budget | `05-budget.md` | When requirements are known |
| 6 | Milestone Plan & Timeline | `06-timeline.md` | After budget scoped |
| 7 | Sustainability Plan | `07-sustainability.md` | Late stage — post-grant story |
| 8 | Appendix & Data | `08-appendix.md` | Accumulate continuously |
| — | Pre-Submission Checklist | `checklist.md` | Final pass before submitting |

## Token-Saving Rules

- **Each section ≤ 150 lines.** If a section exceeds this, split it further.
- **Read only the section you're editing.** Don't load the whole grant.
- **Index file stays under 20 lines.** Just metadata + links.
- **Checklist lives separately** so it's never loaded during drafting.
