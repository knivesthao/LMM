## Application Questions

### Q1: Project Title
[up to 250 characters]

### Q2: Application summary
[up to 650 characters]

Please briefly summarize your proposed innovation and how you would use a DIV Fund grant. Describe: (i) what your innovation is; (ii) who it serves; (iii) what development outcome(s) it aims to improve (e.g., increased consumption, lives saved, improved test scores etc.); and (iv) what activities you propose to conduct with DIV funding.

### Q3: Problem statement
[up to 150 words]

What specific development challenge are you addressing, and who does it affect? Describe the problem you are tackling, how it affects people living in poverty, and which populations are most impacted. Please provide your best estimate of the number of people affected: (i) in your target country/countries; and (ii) globally.

### Q4: Your innovation
[up to 2,300 characters]

What is the core innovation for which you are requesting DIV Fund support? How does your innovation compare to existing alternatives?

Please describe:
(i) What your innovation is and how exactly it works: Describe what your specific product, service, policy, or delivery/financing approach is. Walk us through a typical use case: who is the user, and what specifically happens when they engage with your innovation?
(ii) How it compares to existing alternatives: What are the current solutions, and how and why does your innovation represent a clear improvement over these alternatives?

### Q5: Theory of change
[up to 2,300 characters]

What key development outcomes do you expect your innovation to improve, and how?

Please describe the:
(i) Key measurable outcomes your innovation aims to improve (i.e., increased consumption, lives saved, improved test scores, etc.), and how these directly address the specific development challenge you are focused on; and your
(ii) "Theory of change": Walk us through the specific causal chain from your innovation to impact: what happens first, what changes as a result, and how does that ultimately lead to your target outcomes? Be specific about the mechanism and existing evidence supporting your assumptions.

### Q6: Use of funding
[up to 2,000 characters]

What specific activities will the DIV Fund grant support? Briefly describe the main activities that will be supported by the proposed DIV Fund grant. These activities should correspond to the evidence and learning and/or scaling plans you will describe within this application. Please describe what this DIV grant would make possible that would not otherwise occur.

For each major activity, note: (i) its purpose; (ii) how it contributes to increasing the scale of your innovation or generating important evidence and learning; and (iii) its approximate share of the requested grant budget.

### Q7: Evidence of impact to date
[up to 2,000 characters]

What existing evidence demonstrates that your innovation improves key development outcomes? Summarize any evaluations of your innovation or any related approaches. For each, briefly note: (i) outcomes measured and impact estimates; and (ii) the study design. If causal evidence is not yet available, please describe the strongest relevant evidence supporting your expected impact (e.g., findings from monitoring data, indications of adoption or demand, or operational feasibility).

### Q8: Costs
[up to 2,000 characters]

What does it currently cost to deliver your innovation, and how do you anticipate costs will change as you scale? Provide your best estimate of:

(1) **Current unit cost:** The cost per person reached, showing how this figure is calculated and what costs are included. Include both fixed costs (e.g., overhead, infrastructure) and variable costs (e.g., staff, materials, delivery costs). Please note whether costs are based on data or projections.

(2) **Projected unit cost at scale:** Your projected cost per person at larger scale, including the scale at which this cost estimate applies. Highlight major cost drivers and whether/how you expect these to change as you scale.

### Q9: Potential cost-effectiveness
[250–1,300 characters]

How would your innovation compare to existing approaches in terms of outcomes achieved per dollar spent? Please estimate the cost-effectiveness (cost per outcome or impact per dollar) of your innovation when implemented at scale, and briefly explain the key assumptions and data behind your estimate. What might make your innovation more cost-effective than current solutions or alternatives?

### Q10: Scale
[up to 2,000 characters]

Approximately how many people has your innovation directly reached to date? How many people do you expect your innovation to reach during the grant period, how many people will be reached at scale, and how will this be sustained?

Provide your best estimate of:
(1) **Current scale:** The number of people directly reached to date. Clearly describe how people currently access the innovation, specifying who bears the costs.
(2) **Anticipated scale:** The number of people you anticipate your innovation directly reaching during the proposed grant period, and the number of people you anticipate it will reach at scale. Clearly state the main assumptions and data behind your projections. Clearly describe how you expect people will access the innovation, who will ultimately pay for it, and any evidence supporting their willingness and ability to cover the cost. What existing commitments, demand signals, policy alignment, or market traction suggests the innovation could realistically scale in this manner?

### Q11: Evidence and learning
[up to 1,300 characters]

What are the key questions you will answer during the DIV Fund grant period? Describe the main learning question(s) this funding will address. For each, explain why this question is critical for the next phase of your innovation's development and the methods you will use to answer each question over the course of a grant, including how you will measure relevant outcomes.

**For proposals that contain an impact evaluation:**
[up to 1,100 characters]
If you plan to conduct an impact evaluation, also include: the unit of assignment, description of the treatment and comparison groups, power calculations and minimum detectable effect for the primary outcome(s), the sample and data sources, and the expected timeline.

### Q12: Your organization and partners
[up to 975 characters]

Who will lead and implement this project? Briefly describe your organization and key team members implementing the proposed project, including their relevant expertise and expected level of effort. If applicable, please also describe any partner researchers or implementation organizations, their expected role, and the status of these partnerships (in place or to be developed).

Please also describe any confirmed co-funding, investment, or implementation partnerships supporting this project. Clearly indicate the role of each partner, the nature of their commitment, and whether funding or support is already secured.
