# 2. Problem Statement

Laos faces a dual literacy challenge: low reading proficiency in both Lao and English. Educational materials are scarce, expensive, and rarely culturally relevant. Digital infrastructure is weak — high data costs, intermittent connectivity, and limited digital payment systems create barriers. Girls and ethnic minority groups face additional exclusion from educational resources.

## Target Group

- **Primary:** Lao citizens aged 6-24 in rural areas
- **Secondary:** Ethnic minority communities, girls, and young women
- **Why this group:** Rural and ethnic minority populations in Laos face the greatest barriers to educational materials due to geographic isolation, poverty, and language barriers

## Gender & Social Inclusion

Girls in rural Laos have significantly lower literacy rates (62.9% female vs 78.1% male). Ethnic minority girls face compounded barriers — limited access to schools, lower literacy rates, and lack of culturally-relevant learning materials in their languages.
