# 3. Solution & Hypothesis

## Hypothesis

> *"Providing AI-generated, culturally-relevant comic books in Lao and English through an offline-capable mobile platform will improve reading engagement and comprehension among Lao children aged 6-14, with equal or greater benefit for girls."*

## Solution

A web-based platform where Lao educators and content creators produce AI-assisted comic books and interactive learning materials in both Lao and English. Content is distributed through a mobile-accessible digital library optimized for low-bandwidth environments.

## Design Thinking Approach

**Prototyping (2 months):**
- Work with 10-15 children and 5 teachers in Vientiane
- Test a simple version — hand-drawn comics + basic AI generation
- Observe how children engage with digital stories
- Gender-balanced participant groups

**Testing (4 months):**
- Deploy the full web app to 3 rural schools
- Test comprehension improvements, engagement, and usability
- Iterate based on feedback
- Disaggregate all data by gender

## Gender Equity Plan

- Content created in both Lao and ethnic minority languages
- Stories feature girls and women as protagonists in diverse roles
- Female creators recruited and trained as content publishers
- Platform accessible via QR payment — no bank account needed (critical for women who may not have bank accounts)
