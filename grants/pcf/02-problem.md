# 2. Problem Statement

Only [X]% of Lao children achieve basic reading proficiency by age 10. Rural schools lack books in both Lao and English. Educational materials are dominated by foreign content that does not reflect Lao culture or daily life. Girls in ethnic minority communities face the greatest barriers to accessing quality educational materials.

## Target Group (PCF Focus)

Children aged 4-18 excluded from inclusive, equitable quality education.

## Why This Group

PCF targets children excluded from quality education. In Laos, this encompasses rural children (limited school access), ethnic minority children (language barriers), and girls (lower literacy rates, cultural barriers to education).

## Gender & Social Inclusion

Girls in rural and ethnic minority communities face compounded barriers — limited access to schools, lower literacy rates, and lack of culturally-relevant learning materials. Our solution specifically addresses this by:
- Creating content featuring girls as protagonists in diverse roles
- Ensuring female creators are recruited and trained
- Keeping content affordable to reduce gender-based economic exclusion
